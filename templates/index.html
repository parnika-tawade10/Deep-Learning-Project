What is POS Tagging?

Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging identifies grammatical categories of words in text. It helps understand sentence structure and word relationships.

Common POS Tags:

  • Nouns: People, places, things (dog, table, happiness)
  • Verbs: Actions, states (runs, jumps, thinks, is)
  • Adjectives: Descriptions (quick, brown, lazy, beautiful)
  • Adverbs: Modifiers (quickly, over, gracefully)
  • Pronouns: Replace nouns (he, she, it, they)
  • Prepositions: Relationships (over, under, with, to)
